Installing it

  1. Tap the green button. Your phone will ask if you’re sure, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say it can’t install apps from this source yet; tap Settings and turn the switch on, then press back.
  3. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn you that it doesn’t recognise the app, that just means it didn’t come from the Play Store. Choose Install anyway.
Updating later: come back to this page and install the newest version straight over the old one. Your daily log and settings are kept.
This app tells you what’s in packaged food and how it compares to normal daily limits. It isn’t medical advice, if you have a health condition, your doctor’s guidance comes first. Food data comes from Open Food Facts (ODbL).
